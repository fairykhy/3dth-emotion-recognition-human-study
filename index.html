Human Study for Emotion Recognition of 3D Talking Head Videos

Information and consent

In this study you will watch short, silent videos of AI-generated talking head videos and judge which emotion each face expresses. The study takes about 10 minutes.

  • We record only your answers, response times, and your Prolific ID. No personal information, audio, or video of you is collected.
  • We do not anticipate any risks from taking part beyond those of ordinary computer use.
  • Your responses are stored in a access-controlled research database and are not shared with anyone outside the research team. They will be kept for up to 3 years after the study ends for verification purposes, in line with institutional data-retention policy, and then deleted.
  • Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing this page, without any penalty or loss of benefits; if you stop before finishing, your partial responses are not used.
  • This study will be reviewed by the KAIST Institutional Review Board (IRB).

Instructions

You will see 52 short videos (1–7 seconds each, no sound).

For each video, choose the emotion the face expresses:

  • Happy, Sad, or Angry
  • If none of these fits, choose None of three, then pick the closest one. If nothing is close either, choose Other there and type the emotion you see.

You cannot go back to a previous video once you move on, so please choose carefully.

  • Focus on the facial expression. The mouth moves because the face is speaking; this is not an emotion by itself.
  • Each video plays once in full before the answer buttons unlock, then keeps looping.
  • We recommend to use a desktop rather than a mobile device. Your progress is saved automatically, but please do not edit the page address or open the study in a new tab/window.
